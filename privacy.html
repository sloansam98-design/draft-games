Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 9, 2026

Overview

Draft Games (draftgames.net) provides free mini-games to help fantasy leagues decide draft order. This policy explains what information we collect and how we use it.

Information we collect

We do not ask you to create an account or submit personal information to play our games.

When you visit the site, we use Google Analytics 4 to understand how the site is used — for example, which pages are visited, general location (country/region), browser type, and device type. Google may set cookies or use similar technologies as part of this service.

Team names or league data you enter into a game are processed in your browser to run the game. We do not store that information on our servers.

How we use information

Analytics data helps us improve the site, fix issues, and understand which games people use. We do not sell your personal information.

Third-party services

Google Analytics is provided by Google LLC. Google’s use of data is governed by their policies. You can learn more at Google’s Privacy Policy and opt out of Google Analytics using the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

The site is hosted on Netlify. Netlify may process technical data (such as IP address and request logs) to deliver the site securely.

Children

Draft Games is intended for general audiences. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will reflect the latest version.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, contact the site operator through the Draft Games project repository or your usual channel for site feedback.
